## Verdict
- Gate 0 passes on real AMD hardware: real int4 within 0.013 of simulated int4 on all 3 tasks; never more than noise
  below full (the only |delta| > 0.02 is +0.021 ABOVE full).
- Before the prefill fix (Kaggle T4 run v1): hotpotqa real4 0.367 vs sim 0.419. After: 0.420 vs 0.424.
- Speed: pure-PyTorch dequantization of the whole cache per step -> 5.5-6x slower decode. Claims must be about
  capacity until a fused int4 attention kernel exists; vLLM fp8 KV is the fair speed comparison.
- Sessions on AMD AI Notebooks end after ~30 min (kernel "Disconnected" at 11:59 after launch ~11:30).

## FUSED int4 decode kernel (Triton, kvquant/fused4.py) on MI300X, session 15:24 (amd_gate2.py)
Triton 3.5.1 smoke OK. Kernel vs torch reference: rel max err 6.6e-4 .. 1.4e-3 at n = 128 / 640 / 4096 / 32768,
R = 7 and 4 (all OK).
Per-layer decode attention, Qwen-7B shape (28 q heads, 4 kv heads, D 128), one new token:
| ctx | bf16 SDPA (torch, enable_gqa) | int4 unfused (dequant + SDPA) | int4 FUSED | fused vs unfused | fused vs bf16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4,096 | 191.7 us | 323.4 us | 397.7 us | 0.8x | 0.48x |
| 16,384 | 739.7 us | 965.5 us | 400.6 us | 2.4x | 1.85x |
| 32,768 | 1,470.4 us | 1,917.6 us | 380.6 us | 5.0x | 3.86x |
| 65,536 | 2,930.6 us | 3,790.9 us | 459.7 us | 8.2x | 6.37x |
Caveats: fused time is ~flat ~400 us = fixed overhead (launch, torch tail, merge), shrinkable. The bf16 baseline
is PyTorch SDPA with GQA on ROCm, ~46 GB/s effective (far from MI300X 5.3 TB/s): NOT a tuned flash-decoding
kernel. Claim "5-8x faster than our unfused path; faster than stock PyTorch bf16 attention at long context";
compare vs vLLM fp8 KV separately.
End-to-end decode, Qwen2.5-7B, random-token prompt, 64 new tokens (ms/token):
| ctx | bf16 | int4 unfused | int4 FUSED | fused==unfused tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3,500 | 17.4 | 36.7 | 55.5 | 47 % |
| 32,000 | 51.8 | 64.8 | **47.9** | 100 % |
At 32k fused beats bf16 end to end. At 3.5k the per-layer fixed overhead of the torch glue around the kernel
dominates (fix: CUDA graphs / move the tail + merge into the kernel). 47 % token agreement at 3.5k on a random
prompt = flat logits + fp16-in-kernel vs bf16 rounding; quality on real text checked in step 4.
F1 multifieldqa N=20 (simplified harness, absolute values lower than the KVPress runs): bf16 0.2243 | int4 unfused
0.2266 | int4 FUSED 0.2218 -> the kernel keeps quality.
Capacity with the CONTIGUOUS store (32k ctx/user): bf16 99 | int4 308 (worse than 341 with the list store).
Cause: PyTorch caching allocator serves allocations < 10 MB from 20 MB segments; one 8 MB codes buffer per
layer -> 2 per segment, 4 MB wasted (~20 %). Fix: one >10 MB arena per layer (K+V+scales) or
expandable_segments; re-test pending.
expandable_segments re-test: no change (308, 157.4 GiB) -> not effective on this ROCm build / not the cause alone.
**Per-layer ARENA (one ~17 MiB uint8 block per layer, K+V codes and scales as views): bf16 99 | int4 357 users
(3.61x), 28 tensors/user, OOM at 189.6 GiB (all memory used).** History: list store 341 (3.44x) -> contiguous
308 -> arena 357. Byte ratio 3.76x; remaining gap = 2 MiB rounding of the 17 MiB block (~5.6 %).
